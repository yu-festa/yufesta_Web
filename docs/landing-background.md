Midnight navy and rich cobalt blue starfield, tiny natural silver-blue stars, luminous powder-blue cumulus clouds curling along the bottom and lower side edges, a subtle blue-violet nebula and one graceful thin starlight arc on the far right. Similar atmosphere to a luxurious dreamy cosmic festival poster, restrained lighting, layered cloud detail, tasteful and spacious. Blue dominant with a little lavender, no pink/orange dominant areas. A single cohesive full-bleed background, no panels or borders. High resolution, portrait 1024x1536 composition.
